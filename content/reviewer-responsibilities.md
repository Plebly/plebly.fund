# Reviewer rules

Anyone who finishes a bounty can review later work. Until those seats exist, named bootstrap reviewers hold the roster.

A completion, extension, or listing challenge passes when at least two-thirds of active reviewers vote yes, enough people vote (at least three, or the whole roster up to five), and someone votes yes. Not voting is not a yes.

You cannot vote on your own bounty. The first named seats cannot be removed by a funder vote.

Reviews happen on the project page. The roster and elections are on the Reviewers page.

A listed AI Reviewer, powered by BTCDecoded Intelligence, may analyze bounty work against the spec. It does not vote and never releases funds.
