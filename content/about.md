## About Plebly

Plebly funds Bitcoin work in public. New listings pay the builder. That payment is final. Plebly's server holds no keys.

## What we believe

**Plebly's server holds no keys.** A direct payment goes to the builder and is not held.

**Public listings.** Every project and address is visible.

**Published rules.** Fees and process changes are public. A direct payment takes no platform fee.

## How it works

1. **List:** A bounty for builders or a campaign for a cause.
2. **Donate:** Send Bitcoin to the address on the listing.
3. **Build or receive:** Builders apply, or the organizer receives.
4. **Pay:** A direct payment is already with the builder.

## For builders

Watch to follow — it does not reserve the work. Apply before anyone pays. A bond holds your place. Ship, get reviewed. A direct payment is already final.

## Lightning

A creator can publish a Lightning Address. Donors pay that address. Plebly does not receive it. The endowment, when Lightning is enabled, uses OpenNode.
