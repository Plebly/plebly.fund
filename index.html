Plebly

Fund open Bitcoin work with publicly verifiable on-chain escrow. No custodian in the middle.

How it works

  • Propose Bitcoin development or research in the open.
  • Anyone donates to a public on-chain escrow address.
  • Builders claim funded work once the claim floor is met.
  • Reviewers verify deliverables; keyholders release escrow.
