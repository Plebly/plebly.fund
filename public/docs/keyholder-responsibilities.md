# Keyholder responsibilities

A Plebly keyholder holds an independent Bitcoin key on their own air-gapped hardware, reviews monthly release PSBTs, signs offline, and uploads partial signatures. They stay reachable within seven days of a signing request. They earn two percent of that month’s disbursed bounty total, split equally among the keyholders who signed that month’s PSBT, capped at 500,000 sats per keyholder; the share is recorded on an earnings ledger and paid from the single fee-address output, not as a per-signer output in the release transaction. Until that later cash-out, the operator can spend the keyholder pool sitting on the fee address. If a keyholder goes dark, the seven-day stall runbook applies, the incident goes public, and repeated non-response results in removal via reviewer vote. A keyholder may resign at any time with thirty days’ notice so the platform can update the multisig descriptor before the key is retired. Fulfillers are paid in the monthly batch, not the instant a bounty completes.
